How this site works
  • Pages are what visitors open: articles, categories, student unions, tools and events. Each page has its own address.
  • Snippets are pieces shown inside pages:
    • menus
    • the header message
    • the home page message board

How pages are organised

Everything sits under the root page Artikkler:

  • Informasjonsartikkler → categories (Praktisk, Sosialt …) → articles, tools and student unions
  • Nyheter → categories → news articles
  • Arrangementer → events (an event can hold sub-events)

A page's address follows its place in the tree. Moving a page changes its address; the old one redirects.

Write in Norwegian

Norwegian is the source of truth; there are no separate English pages. When you publish, DeepL fills in the English within about a minute. To check or fix it, use the English button on the page or snippet. Your fixes are kept until the Norwegian text under them changes.
